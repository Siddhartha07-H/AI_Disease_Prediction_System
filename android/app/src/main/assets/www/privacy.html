HealthAI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: Version 2.0.0

CRITICAL MEDICAL APP POLICY & DISCLAIMER: HealthAI is an artificial intelligence-based clinical decision-support application. It does NOT provide certified clinical medical diagnoses, prescribe prescription pharmaceuticals, or replace professional consultation with a licensed doctor. In case of an acute medical emergency, call 911, 112, or local emergency response immediately.

1. Information We Collect

To provide AI-powered disease prediction and appointment booking services, HealthAI collects the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, and encrypted password hash (Argon2id).
  • Health & Symptom Inputs: Self-selected physiological symptoms provided for the purpose of machine learning inference.
  • Health Profile (Optional): Age, biological sex/gender, blood group, allergies, and emergency contact details for personal patient cards.
  • Appointment Data: Chosen medical specialist, clinic location, appointment date and timeslot.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your health data is strictly processed for:

  • Calculating statistical symptom correlations against clinical classification models.
  • Generating individualized printable medical summaries for discussion with certified doctors.
  • Facilitating scheduled appointments with verified medical specialists.
  • Ensuring application account security and unauthorized access prevention.

3. Data Protection, Security & Encryption

We treat health-related data with rigorous security standards:

  • All network transmissions are encrypted end-to-end using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • User passwords are protected with modern salt-and-hash cryptographic hashing algorithms.
  • Health data is NEVER sold, rented, or monetized for advertising purposes.
  • Database systems utilize parameterized queries preventing SQL injection vulnerabilities.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

Users maintain complete ownership of their data. You have the right to:

  • Request an export of all your stored prediction history and appointment logs.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated health records from our databases.
  • Revoke access or modify your health profile information at any time.

5. Google Play Store Medical Apps Policy Compliance

HealthAI adheres strictly to the Google Play Developer Content Policy regarding Health and Medical Applications:

  • We clearly inform users of the informational decision-support nature of this tool.
  • We provide clear attribution for medical precautions and recommend specialist consultations.
  • We do not promote unverified cures or self-treatment of critical life-threatening conditions.
